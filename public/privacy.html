Clear, limited use of your information.

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Information you provide

When you request a strategy call, contact CONCODE, or create an account, we may collect the information you submit, such as your name, email address, phone number, project details, and account details.

How we use it

  • Respond to your request and discuss the services you asked about.

  • Maintain records needed to operate, improve, and protect the service.

Who can access it

Your information is available to the CONCODE team and to service providers used to run the site and deliver requested communications, such as hosting, database, and email-notification providers. We do not sell enquiry information.

Cookies and sessions

CONCODE uses a secure, HttpOnly session cookie after sign-in so the browser can keep you signed in without exposing the session token to page scripts. You can sign out at any time from your workspace.

Your choices

You may ask to review, correct, or delete information you submitted by emailing ms.concode@gmail.com. We may retain limited information where necessary for security, dispute resolution, or other legitimate operational obligations.

Contact
